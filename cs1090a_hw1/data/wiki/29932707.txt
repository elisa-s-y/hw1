'''''News from Home''''' is a 1976<ref>{{cite web |url=https://chantalakerman.foundation/works/news-from-home/ |title=News from Home |publisher=Fondation Chantal Akerman |access-date=23 January 2024}}</ref> [[avant-garde]] [[documentary film]] directed by [[Chantal Akerman]]. The film consists of long takes of locations in [[New York City]] set to Akerman's [[voice-over]] as she reads letters that her mother sent her between 1971 and 1973 when Akerman lived in the city.

==Production==
In November 1971, at the age of 21, Belgian film director Chantal Akerman moved to New York City. There she took petty jobs, made films and befriended filmmakers such as [[Jonas Mekas]] and the cinematographer [[Babette Mangolte]], who became one of her recurring collaborators. According to Akerman, she spent the period living "like a vagabond."<ref name=brilliantdecade>{{cite web |last=Anderson |first=Melissa |date=19 January 2010 |url=http://www.movingimagesource.us/articles/her-brilliant-decade-20100119 |title=Her Brilliant Decade |work=[[Moving Image Source]] |publisher=[[Museum of the Moving Image (New York City)|Museum of the Moving Image]] |access-date=6 December 2010 |url-status=live |archive-date=21 April 2013 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130421180340/http://www.movingimagesource.us/articles/her-brilliant-decade-20100119 }}</ref> Principal photography for ''News from Home'' took place in the summer of 1976, three years after Akerman had returned to Belgium and after she had achieved critical success with the 1975 film ''[[Jeanne Dielman, 23 quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles]]''. Locations chosen for ''News from Home'' correspond to the areas where Akerman used to take walks, which include the [[Times Square – 42nd Street (New York City Subway)|Times Square subway station]] and a long shot driving up [[Tenth Avenue (Manhattan)|Tenth Avenue]] from [[List of numbered streets in Manhattan#30th Street|30th]] to 49th Streets in [[Hell's Kitchen, Manhattan|Hell's Kitchen]]. Other scenes were filmed in [[Tribeca]] and on the [[Staten Island Ferry]]. The sound was not recorded along with the images, but was added later.<ref name=brilliantdecade />

==Home media==
''News from Home'' was released on [[DVD]] in France through Carlotta Films on 18 April 2007.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.evene.fr/cinema/films/news-from-home-8388.php |title=News from home - film de Chantal Akerman |language=French |work=Evene |publisher=[[Le Figaro|Figaromedias]] |access-date=7 December 2010}}</ref> In 2010, [[The Criterion Collection]] released the film on DVD through its "Eclipse" series, as part of a set called ''Chantal Akerman in the Seventies''. The set includes four feature films that Akerman directed in the 1970s as well as a number of short films.<ref>{{cite web |last=Kehr |first=Dave |date=31 January 2010 |title=The Evolving Vision of a Belgian Auteur |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2010/01/31/movies/homevideo/31kehr.html |work=[[The New York Times]] |access-date=6 December 2010}}</ref> ''News from Home'' is included on the first disc of the set, called ''The New York Films''.<ref name=brilliantdecade />

On 23 January 2024, The Criterion Collection released ''News from Home'', along with eight other works by Akerman, on [[Blu-ray]] as part of the box set ''Chantal Akerman Masterpieces (1968–1978)''.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.criterion.com/boxsets/7045-chantal-akerman-masterpieces-19681978 |title=Chantal Akerman Masterpieces, 1968–1978 |website=Criterion.com |publisher=[[The Criterion Collection]] |access-date=23 January 2024}}</ref>

==Reception==
''News from Home'' has a [[List of films with a 100% rating on Rotten Tomatoes|100% rating]] on [[Rotten Tomatoes]], based on 6 reviews.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/news_from_home |title=News From Home &#124; Rotten Tomatoes |website=www.rottentomatoes.com}}</ref> In the ''[[Chicago Reader]]'', [[Jonathan Rosenbaum]] called it "one of the best depictions of the alienation of exile that I know."<ref>{{cite web |url=https://enzian.org/film/news-from-home/ |title=News From Home |website=Enzian Theater}}</ref>
