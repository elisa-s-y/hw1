'''''Hello Beautiful''''' is a 2025 American [[drama film]] written, and directed by Ziad H. Hamzeh, and starring [[Tricia Helfer]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://filmthreat.com/reviews/hello-beautiful/|title= Film Review by Terry Sherwood |website=[[Film Threat]]|date=April 1, 2025|access-date=May 31, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.filmfocusonline.com/post/hello-beautiful-review|title=Review by Romey Norton|date=April 2, 2025|access-date=May 31, 2025}}</ref> The film tells a deeply emotional and inspiring story of resilience, love, and self-discovery, following a supermodel’s battle with [[breast cancer]] at the height of her career.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://cinemacy.com/hello-beautiful-review/|title=Review by Morgan Rojas|date=April 1, 2025|access-date=May 31, 2025}}</ref> The film premiered at [[Beverly Hills Film Festival]] in 2025, where it won the ''Golden Palm Award''.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://beverlyhillsfilmfestival.com/2025-at-a-glance/|title=2025 At a Glance|access-date=May 31, 2025}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
Willow Adair, a celebrated model enjoying the peak of her success, is suddenly diagnosed with breast cancer—sending her world into upheaval. As she navigates illness and personal loss, Willow embarks on a transformative journey of heartbreak, hope, and self-acceptance. Through moments of despair and triumph, she comes to understand what truly matters: the courage to fight, the grace to let go, and the power of embracing oneself. The film explores the resilience of the human spirit, the unbreakable bonds of love, and the courage it takes to rise when life falls apart.
